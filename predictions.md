## 2. My findings

### Finding 1: The weather forecast is not clearly connected to the closing decision

- Where: https://bakery-store-manager-lastbatch-mgmt-umber.vercel.app/, on the Closing List screen, at the weather strip above the list of bakery items.
- What I did, what I saw: I read the weather forecast while deciding whether an unsold item should be marked down 20%, marked down 50%, or pulled. The strip showed the area, time period, and forecast, but nothing on screen explained how the weather should affect any of the three decisions.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match Between the System and the Real World.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already has the forecast. The issue is how that information is presented and connected to the manager's real closing decision.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by how often it happens. Every manager sees the weather strip during the closing workflow and has to work out for themselves whether it matters, although they can still complete the closing list.
- The repair: The screen explains what role the forecast plays in the closing decision and how a manager should interpret it as one additional input, without automatically making the markdown decision for them.

### Finding 2: A first-time manager gets no guidance on choosing 20%, 50%, or Pull

- Where: https://bakery-store-manager-lastbatch-mgmt-umber.vercel.app/, on the Closing List screen, at the 20% off, 50% off, and Pull buttons on each item card.
- What I did, what I saw: I used the closing list as if I were a manager using LastBatch for the first time. I could see the three buttons on every item, but nothing on the screen explained when a manager should choose 20%, 50%, or Pull.
- Which heuristic: 10, Help and Documentation.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already knows the three actions and each item's details, so guidance on what each action means can be added to the interface without anything new from the back end.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by whether the person can learn around it. An experienced manager may already understand the markdown rules, but a first-time or occasional manager may hesitate or rely on knowledge from outside the app.
- The repair: A manager can see, at the moment of choosing, a short explanation of what each action means and when it would normally be appropriate.

### Finding 3: The This Week screen says to bake less, but not how much less

- Where: https://bakery-store-manager-lastbatch-mgmt-umber.vercel.app/, on the This Week screen, at the "Bake Less of These" list of products pulled most often.
- What I did, what I saw: I opened the This Week screen to see how the week's closing decisions should affect the next bake. The screen tells me to review the week to calibrate future bake quantities and lists products to bake less of, including how many units were discarded. It does not say how much less to bake, so I had to interpret the numbers and work out the adjustment myself.
- Which heuristic: 7, Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: Screen. The page already has the units discarded and the number of times each product was pulled, so it can make the information easier and faster for a regular manager to act on without requiring new back-end data.
- Severity, and why: 2, driven by how often it happens. It does not stop the manager from completing the closing task, but a regular manager using this screen for future bake planning has to interpret the figures manually each time.
- The repair: For each product on the "Bake Less of These" list, the manager can quickly understand what the week's pull pattern suggests for the next bake quantity, without having to calculate or infer the adjustment themselves.

## 3. My predictions

1. The three findings I expect my groupmates to raise, and the severity I expect them to give each:
   - Finding 1 at severity 2. The weather strip is visible during the main task, so a first-time reviewer is likely to ask why it is there and how it should affect 20%, 50%, or Pull.
   - Finding 2 at severity 2, although a reviewer who is unfamiliar with bakery closing procedures may rate it 3. It is easy to notice because the three actions are at the centre of the main workflow.
   - Finding 3 at severity 2. A reviewer who opens the This Week screen may understand the figures but still question how those numbers are supposed to change the manager's next bake quantity.

2. The heuristic I think my product breaks worst: 10, Help and Documentation. LastBatch keeps the workflow visually simple, but that simplicity assumes that the manager already understands the closing process, what each markdown choice means, and how secondary information such as weather and weekly history should be interpreted. A new user may therefore be able to operate the interface without fully understanding how to make a good decision. Only Finding 2 is directly logged under heuristic 10, but I expect my groupmates to identify other places where the product assumes knowledge that is not explained clearly on screen.

3. The finding that would show my own evaluation was wrong: If a groupmate reports that when the weather request is slow, the weather area gives no visible loading indication and appears blank or unchanged, under heuristic 1, Visibility of System Status, at severity 3 or higher, my evaluation was wrong. I focused mainly on whether the weather information was useful after it appeared, and I may have missed the waiting state because the forecast loaded successfully and quickly during my own tests.
